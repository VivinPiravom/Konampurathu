Our Story : Our Roots • Our Memories • Our Family • Our Future

Our Roots :

Every family has a story. Some stories are written in books, while others live in the memories, traditions, and stories passed down from one generation to another.The story of the Konampurathu Family begins with its roots in Mylakkulam (Kakkad)(മൈലക്കുളം (കക്കാട്)).From these roots grew a family whose branches spread across different places and generations, carrying with them memories, values, traditions, and relationships. At the heart of the family’s early history was Mathew Mylakkulam (മാത്യു മൈലക്കുളം). He was known for his work in agriculture and medicine (വൈദ്യം), and his life formed an important part of the family’s early heritage.

The Beginning of a Family :

Mathew’s children became the beginning of different branches of the family. Among them was Chacko(ചാക്കോ), who became connected with the Konampurathu Family (കോനാംപുറം കുടുംബം) of Piravom. Chacko married Mariya(മറിയം), a member of the Poovanthungal Family (പൂവത്തുങ്കൽ കുടുംബം) of Namakkuzhi. Together, they became part of the next generation of the Konampurathu family.The other children of Mathew also established their own family connections. Through these different branches, the family became connected with many homes and communities.

More Than Names on a Family Tree :

A family tree is more than a collection of names and dates.Behind every name is a person. Behind every person is a life, a family, a place, and a story. Some stories have been carefully remembered. Others have been lost with time. Some names and details are still waiting to be discovered. That is why preserving the history of the Konampurathu Family is important. Every photograph, memory, document, name, and story can help future generations understand where they came from.

Our Heritage :

The journey of the Konampurathu Family is a journey from one generation to another. From മൈലക്കുളം (കക്കാട്) to പിറവം and other places, the family branches have continued to grow. Each generation has added its own chapter to the story. The family may have many branches today, but they all remain connected by a common heritage and by the generations that came before them. Today, we look back with gratitude at those who came before us. We remember their lives, their struggles, their work, their families, and the values they passed on. And we look forward to the generations yet to come.
This website is a small effort to preserve those memories and bring the family together. It is a place where our family tree can grow, where photographs can be remembered, and where stories can be shared. The roots of our family are in the past. The branches are in the present. And the future belongs to the generations yet to come.
